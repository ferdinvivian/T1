Full Steps to Fix + Make SD Card VisibleStep
1: Open DiskpartPress Win + X > Command Prompt (Admin) or Windows PowerShell
(Admin)Type: diskpart and hit EnterStep
2: Find your card
3. Type: list disk
   Look for the size. Ex: 14GB, 32GB, 64GB. That’s your SD card.
4. Type: select disk X
   Replace X with your card number. Double check it's the right size so you don't delete your PC driveStep 3: Wipe and Recreate
5. Type: clean
   This erases everything on that card
6. Type: convert mbr
7. Type: create partition primary
8. Type: format fs=fat32 quick
   Use format fs=exfat quick if you want to store files >4GB
9. Type: assign
   Windows gives it a drive letter. It will pop up in "This PC"Step 4: Finish
10. Type: exit to close diskpartDone! Check "This PC" and your card will be there.
